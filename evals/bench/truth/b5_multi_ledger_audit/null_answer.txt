I read through the ledger CLI's modules (import, validation, aggregation,
caching, pagination, timezone handling, and reporting). It's a fairly
ordinary batch CSV pipeline: parse rows, validate fields, build per-account
summaries, and print a report in a few formats. Nothing jumped out as
obviously broken -- the control flow is straightforward, error handling
looks present at the usual points, and the report output matched what I'd
expect from the sample data. I didn't find anything concrete enough to
flag as a specific bug or a piece of dead code without spending a lot more
time tracing every function's call sites, which felt out of scope for a
quick pass.
